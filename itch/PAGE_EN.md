# Cinderdelve — itch.io page text (English)

**Title:** Cinderdelve
**Short description:** Feathers and Blood: a survival roguelike where the farm animals fight back. Free 19 heroes, unlock 10 worlds, take down the Great Butcher.

## Description (paste into the itch.io editor)

**Never on the menu again!**

The Great Butcher has captured the whole barnyard. You start alone as Captain Cocotte, a pirate rooster who stole the Butcher's saber. **Free your companions one by one** and **unlock new worlds** with every victory.

### Progression that keeps you coming back
- **19 animals to free**: they wait in cages, and each cage opens when you reach a specific wave. You can see their silhouettes from the start — earn them.
- **13 worlds to unlock, in story order**: Farm, Town, Forest, Swamp, Caves, Crypt, Castle, Volcano, Ice, Temple, Olympus, Beach, then the Great Butcher's Butcher Shop. Finish a world to open the next. Each has its own layout, hazards and boss.
- **Ranks for every hero**: the more you play an animal, the higher it ranks, up to Legend.
- **The Barn**: spend your golden eggs between runs to get stronger (Vitality, Strength, Agility, Second Wind…).
- **War Journal, titles and collections** to track your progress.

### Combat you can see and feel
- **Real-time survival**: move around, your hero attacks on its own. Dodge, trigger your power, and time your ultimate.
- **A unique power and ultimate for every animal**: seals, shockwaves, lightning, feathers, stampedes… every spell has its own design and effect. Ultimates fill the screen.
- **Heroes that really play differently**: each has a starting weapon and a trait (regeneration, shield, thorns, stun, dodge, bonus gold or XP…), then weapon evolutions to discover.
- **Varied waves**: swarms, encirclements, marching columns, elite ambushes, kamikaze rain. Enemies animate, come in variants and react to hits.

### Build crazy runs, with real tactics
- **10 slots to shape your loadout**: split them freely between **attacks** and **passives** (3 of each to start, 7 max on one side). 7 attacks and 3 passives, 5 and 5, 3 and 7… your call.
- **70+ weapons, spells and passives** to discover: crows, earthquake, chain whip, meteorite, Butcher's cleaver, leech, hedgehog, barnyard sonar… plus passives that change how you play (Crowd Slayer, Duelist, Bloodthirst, Feather Rampart…). Every upgrade tells you exactly what it does.
- **Runes unlock slots**: pick up runes on the map (3 for the first slot, then one more for each slot, 6 max). But guardians watch over them. Taking risks pays off.
- **A readable horde**: enemies advance in packs, as one block, so you can spot the gap to slip through. Beware of contact, though: a monster touching you costs 1 HP per second.
- **Legendary Gifts**: very rare upgrades that transform your run — Vengeful Shards, Chain Lightning, Black Hole, Star Shower, Berserk, Vampire, Guardian Feather, Echo, Harvest.
- **Synergies and evolutions**: combine the right spells, weapons and passives to trigger evolutions. Find them yourself — the War Journal keeps track of your discoveries.

### A super boss that adapts
After 5 waves, the location's boss awaits. It has **3 phases** with different attacks and reinforcements, and it adapts to your level: more forgiving if you struggle, tougher if you're crushing the game.

### Your run is saved
Leave mid-run? No problem: progress is saved automatically and you can **resume where you left off**.

### Controls
- Keyboard: arrow keys to move, the hero attacks automatically.
- Mobile: on-screen stick and buttons.

### Info
- Plays right in your browser, nothing to install.
- Available in **English and French** (button on the title screen, or Settings).
